# Which shipped sinks must own their chain's checkpoint, checked by
# scripts/check-sink-ownership.py against every class in include/, src/ and
# impls/ that derives from Sink<...> or CommittingSink<...>.
#
#   <path>:<Class> owns
#       The sink declares Sink::gates_checkpoint_ack() (it writes out at the
#       barrier what it received before it) or Sink::stages_state_at_barrier()
#       (a two-phase-commit sink; every CommittingSink). Its guarantee holds
#       only while the checkpoint's acknowledgement follows its barrier hook,
#       so Dag::add_sink refuses another sink beside it.
#
#   <path>:<Class> free <reason>
#       The sink declares neither, and the reason says why its output needs no
#       acknowledgement ordering: it writes nothing outside the job, the
#       barrier travels on to a subtask that acknowledges for it, or it claims
#       nothing a checkpoint could cover.
#
# A new sink class fails the check until it is listed here.

# Sinks that finish their output only at close (a cancelled task's close
# included) and keep no checkpoint state: no checkpoint covers any of their
# output, so acknowledgement ordering changes nothing. Their pages state what
# a failure, a cancel and a restore leave behind.
include/clink/connectors/parquet_sink.hpp:ParquetSink free finishes its file only at close; a checkpoint covers none of its output
impls/s3/include/clink/connectors/parquet_s3_sink.hpp:ParquetS3Sink free finishes its objects only at close; a checkpoint covers none of its output
impls/gcs/include/clink/connectors/parquet_gcs_sink.hpp:ParquetGcsSink free finishes its objects only at close; a checkpoint covers none of its output
impls/azure/include/clink/connectors/parquet_azure_sink.hpp:ParquetAzureSink free finishes its blobs only at close; a checkpoint covers none of its output
impls/webhdfs/include/clink/connectors/webhdfs_parquet_sink.hpp:WebHdfsParquetSink free uploads its file only at close; a checkpoint covers none of its output

# A sink that writes by size and at the end of input, never at a barrier, and
